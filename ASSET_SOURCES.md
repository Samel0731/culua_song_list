# CULUA Fan Archive — asset sources

Reviewed 2026-10-07.

- Profile facts: https://rkmusic.jp/artist/284/ (original short adaptations in Chinese, Japanese, and English).
- Curated original MVs: https://rkmusic.jp/release/610/ links to IdiznKoZBnQ, Hx1KAdapT1M, tXPQo3HHAi4, AqTecLnlcOA. The collection is selected works, not an exhaustive release catalog.
- Music images: YouTube video thumbnails from i.ytimg.com, accompanying the corresponding video playback. No thumbnail downloads or repackaging of official artwork.
- Playback: official YouTube iframe API. No audio extraction.
- Archive: the existing published Google Sheet CSV endpoint, refreshed through Next.js caching every 30 minutes.
- Guideline checked: https://rkmusic.jp/guideline/. Direct reuse of official character artwork was not established as permitted for this design; the hero therefore uses original code-based typography and lighting. No official character image or logo was copied. The site identifies itself as unofficial in the header branding, hero, footer, metadata and About page.
- Social preview: original code-generated typography and lighting, generated by app/opengraph-image.tsx.
